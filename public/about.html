Our story

Jamia Arabia Abdullah Bin Masood was established in 2011 during difficult circumstances. It was started with great effort, and by the grace and mercy of Allah Almighty, it has continued to progress day by day.

What began as a small effort on Bhiria Road is now home to Quran classes, the full Alim course, a girls’ madrasa and a recognised school.

The main branch teaches boys from their first Qaida lesson through Hifz and the eight years of Dars-e-Nizami. Jamia Arabia Aisha Siddiqa brings the same education to girls in a separate branch, and Iqra Islamia Public School gives young children a strong academic start in an Islamic setting.

2011١٤٣٢ هـ

The foundation stone of the Jamia was laid in 2011, corresponding to 1432 AH, at Ward No. 3, Fateh Pur Bhiria Road, District Naushahro Feroze.

Background and need

These Makatib were established in underserved and remote communities, where:

  • people lacked access to basic Quranic education,
  • communities lacked sufficient access to congregational prayer, and
  • mosques were deserted or underused.

یہ مبارک مکاتب ان پسماندہ اور دور افتادہ علاقوں میں قائم کیے گئے ہیں جہاں اہل علاقہ قرآن کریم کی بنیادی تعلیم سے محروم تھے، لوگ پانچ وقت کی باجماعت نماز سے ناآشنا تھے، اور مساجد ویران اور غیر آباد پڑی تھیں۔

٣

The reflection of Quranic light

Through these centres:

  • mosques have become active,
  • Quran recitation has spread through the communities,
  • children and adults have gained religious education, and
  • communities have grown closer to the teachings of the Quran.

ان مراکز کے قیام سے نہ صرف مساجد آباد ہو چکی ہیں، بلکہ گھر گھر تلاوتِ قرآن کی آوازیں گونج رہی ہیں اور پورا ماحول دینی نور سے منور ہو رہا ہے۔

Why “Abdullah Bin Masood”

The Jamia is named after Abdullah ibn Mas’ud (may Allah be pleased with him), one of the earliest people to accept Islam and among the closest companions of the Prophet ﷺ. He was known for his beautiful recitation and deep understanding of the Quran.

The Prophet ﷺ said: “Take the Quran from four,” and he named Abdullah ibn Mas’ud first (Sahih al-Bukhari 3758). Later, as a teacher in Kufa, he trained a generation of scholars whose learning shaped Islamic jurisprudence.

His life of learning the Quran, living by it and teaching it to others is the example this Jamia hopes to follow.

Mission and vision

Our vision: a new generation adorned with the Quran and Sunnah, with every mosque in our towns and villages full and alive. Our mission: to bring correct Quran education, prayer and good character to every child we can reach.

The Quran, read correctly

Every child learns to recite with Tajweed, and many go on to memorise the whole Quran.

Scholars for the community

The Dars-e-Nizami course prepares Ulama who can teach, lead prayer and guide people in their deen.

Education for girls

Jamia Arabia Aisha Siddiqa gives daughters of the area the same depth of religious learning.

Character and adab

Students are taught manners, respect for parents and teachers, and honesty in daily life.

Modern schooling

Iqra Islamia Public School keeps children on a recognised academic path from Nursery to Middle.

Serving Naushahro Feroze

The Jamia is part of the life of its town, open to families who want a sound Islamic education.
