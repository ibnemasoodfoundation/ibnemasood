Started in hard times, growing by Allah’s mercy

Jamia Arabia Abdullah Bin Masood was established in 2011 during difficult circumstances. It was started with great effort, and by the grace and mercy of Allah Almighty, it has continued to progress day by day.

Today the Jamia runs Quran and Dars-e-Nizami classes for boys at the main branch, a dedicated girls’ madrasa, Jamia Arabia Aisha Siddiqa, and the government-recognised Iqra Islamia Public School for children from Nursery to Middle.

نصاب

The path of a student

Every student begins with the letters of the Quran. Those who continue go on to complete the Alim course.

Quran departments

  1. QaidaArabic letters, sounds and joining, the foundation of correct recitation
  2. NazraReading the full Quran from the mushaf with Tajweed
  3. Hifz-ul-QuranMemorising the complete Quran under qualified Qaris
  4. GardaanConjugation of Arabic verbs, preparing students for Dars-e-Nizami

Dars-e-Nizami (Alim course)

  1. Darja-e-UlaFirst year: Arabic grammar, morphology and fiqh basics
  2. Darja-e-Saniya to Darja-e-SadisaYears two to six: tafsir, fiqh, usul, Arabic literature and logic
  3. Mauqoof AlaihSeventh year: preparing for the books of Hadith
  4. Daura-e-HadithFinal year: the six books of Hadith, completing the Alim qualification
