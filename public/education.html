Iqra Islamia Public School

اقراء اسلامیہ پبلک اسکول

A government-recognised school from Nursery through Middle. Children study the regular curriculum alongside Nazra, du’as and Islamic manners, so they never have to choose between school and deen.

The school runs for both the boys’ and girls’ sides of the Jamia.

Government recognisedNursery to Middle

Classes

  1. Nursery & PrepEarly years: reading readiness, numbers and du’as
  2. PrimaryClass 1 to 5
  3. MiddleClass 6 to 8
داخلہ

Admissions

New admissions usually begin in Shawwal for the madrasa and with the academic year for the school. Contact the office to confirm dates and seat availability.

Get in touch

Message us on WhatsApp or call 0300 394 8748 with the student’s age and the class you want.

Placement

Students are placed after a short meeting and a reading test with the teachers.

Bring documents

B-Form or CNIC copy, previous result card if any, and recent photographs.
